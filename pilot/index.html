    About your data

    Signed-in entries are saved under your existing LasO user ID. No raw voice recording is collected. Photos are saved privately with your journal. When you enable sharing, Reginald can read your journal, goals and photos. You can revoke future access at any time. Photo estimates are processed by the configured AI provider when you request them. Estimates are not measurements. Links to trial sessions are not enabled yet. This pilot does not send text alerts. Optional Behavior & Context check-ins and App-E-Tight-only foreground time are separate, off by default, visible only to the account owner, and can be deleted without touching the food journal. The app does not collect phone-wide screen time, other app activity, exact GPS location, food-store proximity, or messages.
