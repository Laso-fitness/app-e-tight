About your data

Signed-in entries are saved under your existing LasO user ID. No raw voice recording is collected. Photos are saved privately with your journal. When you enable sharing, Reginald can read your journal, goals and photos. You can revoke future access at any time. Photo estimates are processed by the configured AI provider when you request them.
